What was holding organic growth back

When we analyzed IV Better’s website and search data, we found five opportunities that kept the brand from reaching its full organic potential.

  • High-volume health queries were underused

    Searches about hydration safety, IV usage for kids, and daily intake limits were generating impressions, but the content wasn’t optimized to dominate those topics.

  • E-E-A-T signals weren’t maximized

    As a medical service brand, IV Better needed stronger authority signals to compete in sensitive health results: structured medical content, expertise positioning and trust reinforcement.

  • Content didn’t match search intent

    Many visitors were asking safety and educational questions, but the content structure didn’t align with Google’s preference for clear, direct, medically structured answers.

  • Topical clusters were thin

    Core services like IV hydration, medical infusions and wellness injections weren’t supported by interconnected articles that build topical authority.

  • Click-through rates had room to improve

    Despite strong impression numbers, click-through rates could improve with better headline structure and search-focused meta optimization.

The demand was already there.

Thousands of people were searching for these hydration and safety questions every month. The real issue was that IV Better wasn’t yet positioned to capture and dominate that demand at scale.

How we captured that demand

Five workstreams to capture high-value search traffic and build lasting visibility in the hydration and wellness space.

  • Service and location pages

    We created a dedicated page for each core service (IV hydration, medical infusions and wellness injections) and an individual page for each location, for strong local visibility.

  • Content built around search intent

    We optimized existing content and added new articles targeting high-volume health queries, safety concerns and educational topics, matching what users were actively searching for.

  • E-E-A-T and authority signals

    We strengthened expertise, authoritativeness and trust by structuring medical content clearly, highlighting professional credentials and adding trust-focused site elements.

  • Meta and CTR optimization

    We improved headlines, meta descriptions and on-page structure to increase click-through rates on high-impression queries.

  • Local credibility and topical authority

    We built backlinks from relevant, reputable sources and interlinked related content to strengthen the site’s topical authority in wellness and IV therapy.

We didn’t promise overnight rankings. We built a strong SEO foundation and helped IV Better grow steadily and sustainably.

The results

Found on Google 2.57 million+ times in one year.

After implementing a structured SEO strategy, IV Better saw strong and measurable growth in Google organic search.

  • 2.57M impressions and 21.2K organic clicks from Google
  • Significant growth in high-intent hydration and wellness search queries
  • Strong visibility across service and location-based searches

Three months against the same period a year earlier

In the 3-month comparison, clicks grew from 731 to 5.32K and impressions from 161K to 794K. Average position improved from 16.2 to 8, and average CTR rose from 0.5% to 0.7%.
